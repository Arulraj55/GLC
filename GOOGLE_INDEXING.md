# Green Link Commerce — Google indexing

The project is configured for the official brand name **Green Link Commerce (GLC)**.

## After deploying
1. Open `https://glc-1.onrender.com/robots.txt` and confirm it loads.
2. Open `https://glc-1.onrender.com/sitemap.xml` and confirm it loads.
3. Add the exact site URL to Google Search Console.
4. Use URL Inspection on `https://glc-1.onrender.com/` and choose **Request Indexing**.

The website has also been given a canonical URL, description, Open Graph metadata, and Schema.org `WebSite` structured data for the Green Link Commerce brand.

Indexing and ranking are controlled by Google and are not guaranteed immediately after deployment.
